## Abstract

As part of the theme "Development of content that widely presents the outcomes of Q-LEAP and other programs and lets people experience them firsthand," this reports a talk and hands-on event held at EXPO 2025 Osaka, Kansai, Japan ("Entangled Moment: [Quantum, Ocean, Space] x Art," August 18, 2025). Exhibits included quantum sensors using diamond NV centers and intracellular temperature sensing, and participants of all ages, nearly 80 in total, took part in a puzzle-solving workshop using a device for finding diamonds with NV centers, which helped bring the outcomes of Q-LEAP to the general public.

(A part of) this research was conducted under the Cross-ministerial Strategic Innovation Promotion Program (SIP) 3rd Phase "Promoting the Application of Advanced Quantum Technology Platforms to Social Issues" by the Council for Science, Technology and Innovation, Cabinet Office (Managing Agency: QST).
