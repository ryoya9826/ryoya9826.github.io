## Abstract

In recent years, homological algebraic studies of modules over not-necessarily-Noetherian rings have become increasingly important in commutative algebra. In this talk, I will introduce generalizations of concepts such as regular sequences and grade, along with their applications. Furthermore, I will present results obtained by the speaker and propose related problems.
