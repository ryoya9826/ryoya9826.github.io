## Abstract

This introduces the industrial applications of quantum information technology to the general public, using quantum sensors that utilize diamonds with NV centers as an example.

(A part of) this research was conducted under the Bridging Research and Development and Society 5.0 Program (BRIDGE) "Development and Trial of a Quantum Human Resources Education Ecosystem" by the Council for Science, Technology and Innovation, Cabinet Office (Managing Agency: QST).
