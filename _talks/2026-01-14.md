## Abstract
In recent commutative ring theory, the importance of homological algebraic considerations for modules
over rings that are not necessarily Noetherian has been increasing year by year. In this talk, I will introduce
generalizations of concepts such as regular sequences and grade, along with their applications and associated
problems.
